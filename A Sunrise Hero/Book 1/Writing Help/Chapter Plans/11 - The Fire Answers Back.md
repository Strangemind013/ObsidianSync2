**Chapter number and working title:** Chapter 11 — The Fire Answers Back  
**POV:** Crow — proposed prisoner POV for witnessing the first Grin fight.  
**Time and location:** The site where Grin confronted the Smokesons; directly after Chapter 10.  
**Essential characters:** Crow, Caide, Grin, the crew.  
**Approximate word budget:** 3,300 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

#### Purpose
**Main contribution to the book:** Expose the gap between stopping ordinary spread and opposing the being causing it.  
**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** The suspension of firefighting and search for powerful allies would lack urgency.

#### Character motivation
**Immediate goal:** Crow: survive the confrontation and keep the crew together.  
**Underlying motive:** Escape being helpless in another nightmare; his specific history remains undefined.  
**Stakes:** The crew's lives and its newly earned hope.

#### Dramatic question
**Question driving the chapter:** Can Caide protect them against the source of the fires?  
**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** He can buy an escape, but cannot defeat Grin.

#### Opposition and tension
**External resistance:** Grin's Warmth mastery overwhelms their assumptions.  
**What the opposing character wants:** Make the crew stop interfering and demonstrate the cost of resistance.  
**Internal pressure or competing desire:** Crow wants to flee, but leaving alone would abandon the first group beginning to feel like his own.

#### Causal sequence
**Starting situation:** Grin demands an end to the firefighting.  
**Trigger:** Caide resists or tries to establish what they are facing.  
**First approach:** The crew reaches for its normal rescue procedures.  
**Complication:** Grin makes those procedures inadequate and nearly kills Caide.  
**Response:** Crow follows or passes a withdrawal instruction instead of freezing or bolting alone.  
**Turning point:** They escape because Caide buys time; the operation must stop.

#### Decision and action
**Options the character perceives:** Flee alone, attack ineffectively, or help execute the withdrawal.  
**Cost or risk of each option:** Flight abandons others; attacking wastes lives; coordination keeps Crow near danger.  
**Decision:** Proposed: help another man withdraw.  
**Action taken:** Carry a warning, open a route, or retrieve an injured teammate; choose one action.  
**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** Assessment and the decision to seek help follow in Chapter 12.

#### Outcome and change
**Practical result:** The crew survives a decisive defeat.  
**Before to after:** Successful firefighters → people forbidden to use their successful method.  
**Character interpretation:** Crow sees courage as coordinated action under fear, not absence of fear.  
**Relationship effect:** Caide's protection earns trust even as his limits become undeniable.  
**Next consequence:** The city's fires continue without the Smokesons' intervention.

#### Reader knowledge and continuity
**What the reader must understand:** Caide barely survives; this is not a near-victory against an evenly matched opponent.  
**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** Crow knows observable danger, not Grin's pain economy or resurrection purpose.  
**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** Grin's full strength and plans remain unknown because he has no reason to explain them.  
**Earlier setup paid off:** The first firebreak and the team's public reputation.  
**Later event prepared:** The need for Tahan, Sol's suppression method, and the later collective capture.  
**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** Grin manifests Warmth; coats buy limited protection and do not make the crew safe against him.

#### Scene list
1. **Location:** Confrontation site | **Objective:** Keep the crew alive | **Resistance:** Grin defeats Caide's initial defense | **Outcome:** The men must withdraw.
2. **Location:** Escape route | **Objective:** Get everyone out | **Resistance:** Fire, injury, and panic | **Outcome:** A small coordinated action saves a teammate.

#### Closing beat
**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** Crow counts survivors while the fires they have been ordered to abandon continue behind them.

#### One sentence summary
Because Grin personally forbids the firefighting, Crow watches Caide challenge him, then helps the crew escape a defeat that leaves the city without its new defense.
