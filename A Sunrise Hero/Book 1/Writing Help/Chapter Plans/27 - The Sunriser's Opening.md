**Chapter number and working title:** Chapter 27 — The Sunriser's Opening  
**POV:** Caide.  
**Time and location:** The same night battlefield; continuous with Chapter 26.  
**Essential characters:** Caide, Seyla, Sol, his companions, the Smokesons, the Faceless.  
**Approximate word budget:** 3,000 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

#### Purpose
**Main contribution to the book:** Pay off Caide's skill, acceptance of dependence, and the team's rehearsed cooperation in one shared success.  
**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** His sacrifice would not lead to a changed way of achieving the heroism he values.

#### Character motivation
**Immediate goal:** Give Seyla the brief light she needs without sacrificing the people holding the target.  
**Underlying motive:** Be useful within the team instead of becoming its whole solution.  
**Stakes:** The crew's lives and the narrow chance to kill the creature.

#### Dramatic question
**Question driving the chapter:** Can Caide create a safe enough instant for Seyla to finish the fight?  
**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** Yes, when Sol coordinates withdrawal and Seyla takes the shot.

#### Opposition and tension
**External resistance:** The hold cannot last indefinitely, and the flash itself could endanger allies.  
**What the opposing character wants:** The creature strains to escape; allies need an exact warning and timing.  
**Internal pressure or competing desire:** Caide is tempted toward a theatrical last stand but must make the signal matter.

#### Causal sequence
**Starting situation:** Seyla defines the light required.  
**Trigger:** Caide sees a way to generate a brief flash or lightning event.  
**First approach:** He establishes the focus and tells her when to fire.  
**Complication:** The crew still occupies the danger area.  
**Response:** He gives Sol the rehearsed fist-over-shoulder signal and waits for coordination.  
**Turning point:** The flash lights the target; Seyla's beam destroys it.

#### Decision and action
**Options the character perceives:** Trigger immediately or trust the team's withdrawal timing.  
**Cost or risk of each option:** Immediate action could kill allies; waiting risks the hold failing.  
**Decision:** Coordinate and accept that another person completes the kill.  
**Action taken:** Signal, position himself, cast the light-producing sequence, and rely on Seyla.  
**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** The arrival of more Faceless opens Chapter 28's unresolved larger response.

#### Outcome and change
**Practical result:** One Faceless is definitively destroyed.  
**Before to after:** Hero trying to be sufficient alone → hero whose contribution enables others.  
**Character interpretation:** Caide can recognize himself in the result without owning every part of it.  
**Relationship effect:** Mutual trust becomes observable action.  
**Next consequence:** More Faceless prevent the single kill from ending the citywide crisis.

#### Reader knowledge and continuity
**What the reader must understand:** Everyone's practiced roles work; the new enemies do not retroactively invalidate that achievement.  
**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** Caide knows the plan and its risk; avoid hiding his actual intended maneuver for surprise.  
**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** More Faceless can be a genuine external development if their existence is established elsewhere.  
**Earlier setup paid off:** Retreat signal, ballista, seal chain, Seyla's beam, and the Sunriser title.  
**Later event prepared:** Dragon-scale escalation and the later quiet reconciliation.  
**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** Maximum three Words per spell. Replace the old 'Heat' label with Warmth; check how a suspended focus and lightning are cast in separate steps.

#### Closing beat
**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** The creature falls to their combined work; then they see the next ones coming.

#### One sentence summary
Because Seyla needs a moment of intense light, Caide trusts Sol to clear the team and creates the opening that lets her beam destroy the Faceless.
