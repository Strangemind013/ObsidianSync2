**Chapter number and working title:** Chapter 6 — Sweet-tooth's Unexpected Rescue  
**POV:** Sweet-tooth.  
**Time and location:** Sadampo prison, a covered cart, and the woods outside Meroun; night.  
**Essential characters:** Sweet-tooth, Dan, Caide, Harum, Batch, Duck, Crow, Lon, Pao.  
**Approximate word budget:** 2,800 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

#### Purpose
**Main contribution to the book:** Introduce the prisoners as individuals through Sweet-tooth's mistaken expectations and their actual choice to join.  
**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** The crew would begin as names on a roster rather than people with different reasons to risk themselves.

#### Character motivation
**Immediate goal:** Escape prison and discover who arranged his release.  
**Underlying motive:** Believe his father still considers him worth rescuing.  
**Stakes:** Freedom, safety, and the humiliating possibility that nobody came for him personally.

#### Dramatic question
**Question driving the chapter:** Is Sweet-tooth being rescued by his father?  
**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** No; he is being offered dangerous work, which he nevertheless chooses.

#### Opposition and tension
**External resistance:** He is hooded, bound, uninformed, and delivered to strangers.  
**What the opposing character wants:** Dan wants a crew; Caide wants compliance without chaos.  
**Internal pressure or competing desire:** Sweet-tooth's pride resists admitting he has no better prospect.

#### Causal sequence
**Starting situation:** Sweet-tooth assumes the extraction is his father's doing.  
**Trigger:** The cart stops in the woods with six other prisoners.  
**First approach:** He waits for the explanation he expects.  
**Complication:** Dan offers firefighting service rather than a homecoming.  
**Response:** The prisoners question pay, danger, and pardons; Sweet-tooth reassesses.  
**Turning point:** He accepts the same offer as the others.

#### Decision and action
**Options the character perceives:** Return to prison or join the fire crew.  
**Cost or risk of each option:** Prison is familiar misery; service risks divine fire and uncertain freedom.  
**Decision:** Join rather than cling to the imagined rescue.  
**Action taken:** Give his agreement and depart with the seven-man crew.  
**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** The first attempt at firefighting follows in Chapter 7.

#### Outcome and change
**Practical result:** All seven join under an arrangement already covered by the authorities.  
**Before to after:** Imagined favored son → one recruit among other desperate men.  
**Character interpretation:** Sweet-tooth can protect his pride with jokes, but his expectation has changed.  
**Relationship effect:** Dan earns conditional cooperation; Caide remains intimidating.  
**Next consequence:** Distinct personalities enter a common enterprise.

#### Reader knowledge and continuity
**What the reader must understand:** This is a covered extraction, not the start of a manhunt.  
**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** Sweet-tooth misunderstands why he was brought out until Dan explains.  
**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** His father's feelings need not be resolved here; do not manufacture a mother-death or home confrontation.  
**Earlier setup paid off:** Dan's dossiers and release arrangements.  
**Later event prepared:** The crew's later argument that risking their lives should earn real freedom.  
**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** The Summer God's taboo makes this offer unusually dangerous; distinguish promised advocacy from guaranteed pardons.

#### Scene list
1. **Location:** Prison and cart | **Objective:** Understand his rescue | **Resistance:** Darkness and no answers | **Outcome:** Sweet-tooth supplies his own flattering explanation.
2. **Location:** Woods | **Objective:** Secure freedom on acceptable terms | **Resistance:** Dan's dangerous offer and the others' questions | **Outcome:** All seven choose service.

#### Closing beat
**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** Sweet-tooth leaves with the crew, still trying to make joining sound like his idea.

#### One sentence summary
Because Sweet-tooth mistakes a covered prison extraction for his father's rescue, Dan's firefighting offer disappoints him, but he chooses the crew over returning to his cell.
