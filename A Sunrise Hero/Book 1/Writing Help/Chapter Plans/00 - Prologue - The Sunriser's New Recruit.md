**Chapter number and working title:** Prologue — The Sunriser's New Recruit  
**POV:** An unnamed recruit; identity remains open.  
**Time and location:** Ten years before the main story, during the Midnight War; exact battlefield open.  
**Essential characters:** Caide, the recruit, a hostile Sage, other soldiers.  
**Approximate word budget:** 2,000 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

#### Purpose
**Main contribution to the book:** Establish the confident, formidable Caide whom the present-day Caide measures himself against.  
**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** Lose the contrast between his old mastery and his current belief that he is useless.

#### Character motivation
**Immediate goal:** Recruit: survive the encounter and follow Caide's orders.  
**Underlying motive:** Find safety and belonging under a legendary fighter.  
**Stakes:** The recruit's life; the unit's survival.

#### Dramatic question
**Question driving the chapter:** Can this recruit survive a Sage encounter under the Sunriser's command?  
**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** Proposed: yes, because Caide reads the danger and decisively defeats the Sage.

#### Opposition and tension
**External resistance:** A Sage whose specific attack defeats ordinary military tactics.  
**What the opposing character wants:** Stop or destroy the unit; personal allegiance remains open.  
**Internal pressure or competing desire:** The recruit's awe makes obedience difficult when an order seems suicidal.

#### Causal sequence
**Starting situation:** The recruit joins Caide expecting a legend.  
**Trigger:** A hostile Sage blocks the unit's immediate objective.  
**First approach:** The recruit follows conventional training.  
**Complication:** Magic makes that response inadequate.  
**Response:** Caide gives a precise instruction and uses swordsmanship with sagecraft.  
**Turning point:** The recruit obeys; Caide creates and exploits the opening.

#### Decision and action
**Options the character perceives:** Trust Caide's order or seek apparently safer cover.  
**Cost or risk of each option:** Obedience requires stepping toward danger; hesitation leaves the recruit exposed.  
**Decision:** Proposed: trust the order.  
**Action taken:** Move on Caide's signal and survive his finishing maneuver.  
**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** The encounter resolves here; the ten-year jump follows.

#### Outcome and change
**Practical result:** Caide wins the encounter and brings the recruit through.  
**Before to after:** Legend heard about → competence witnessed.  
**Character interpretation:** The recruit sees a man who appears able to solve anything.  
**Relationship effect:** Caide earns admiration through skill and personal warmth.  
**Next consequence:** Present-day Caide will struggle against this remembered standard.

#### Reader knowledge and continuity
**What the reader must understand:** Caide once combined two blades, confidence, and magic effectively.  
**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** The recruit cannot see the insecurity that will develop after Caide's injury.  
**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** The wider politics of the war remain outside this recruit's knowledge; do not falsely describe all Sages as extinct.  
**Earlier setup paid off:** No earlier setup required.  
**Later event prepared:** Caide's pride, his title, and the later contrast between solo prowess and teamwork.  
**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** Visible inscriptions; self-augmentation; Break along a blade. Do not combine Break and Endure in one spell.

#### Closing beat
**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** The recruit sees the Sunriser at his apparent height; cut to the diminished present-day man.

#### One sentence summary
Because a Sage threatens the unit, a new recruit tries to survive by following Caide, whose decisive intervention establishes the heroic standard Caide will later struggle to reclaim.
