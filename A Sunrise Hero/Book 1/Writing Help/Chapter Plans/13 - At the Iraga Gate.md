**Chapter number and working title:** Chapter 13 — At the Iraga Gate  
**POV:** Caide.  
**Time and location:** Tahan Iraga's estate: gate, receiving room, then training ground.  
**Essential characters:** Caide, Dan, Tahan Iraga, a steward; Sol may appear.  
**Approximate word budget:** 2,600 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

#### Purpose
**Main contribution to the book:** Bring Caide face to face with a powerful mirror of his old martial identity.  
**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** Tahan's judgment and Sol's participation would lack an earned point of entry.

#### Character motivation
**Immediate goal:** Obtain Tahan's help against Grin.  
**Underlying motive:** Recover usefulness by bringing something Dan's negotiation cannot supply.  
**Stakes:** The city, the crew, and Caide's vulnerable sense of competence.

#### Dramatic question
**Question driving the chapter:** Can Dan and Caide gain help from a man who does not respect Dan's standing?  
**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** They gain a hearing, but assistance becomes conditional on Caide's demonstration.

#### Opposition and tension
**External resistance:** Gatekeeping, Tahan's contempt, and his appetite for a worthy fight.  
**What the opposing character wants:** Tahan wants proof of competence and the stimulation of facing Caide.  
**Internal pressure or competing desire:** Caide needs help but instinctively turns the request into a personal test.

#### Causal sequence
**Starting situation:** Dan expects negotiation to open the estate.  
**Trigger:** The gate refuses him meaningful access.  
**First approach:** Caide supplies a phrase or reference tied to his military past.  
**Complication:** Inside, Tahan dismisses Dan's arguments or standing.  
**Response:** Caide changes the terms by invoking shared martial experience.  
**Turning point:** Tahan challenges him to a duel.

#### Decision and action
**Options the character perceives:** Leave after the insult, continue pleading, or accept the test.  
**Cost or risk of each option:** Leaving means no help; pleading gives Tahan control; fighting risks injury and humiliating defeat.  
**Decision:** Accept with a concrete purpose, rather than fighting only for pride.  
**Action taken:** Agree the duel's terms and step onto the ground.  
**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** The duel and Dan's separate negotiation continue in Chapter 14.

#### Outcome and change
**Practical result:** They have access and a possible route to assistance.  
**Before to after:** Blocked petitioners → guests facing a martial condition.  
**Character interpretation:** Caide reads the challenge as a chance to be recognized again.  
**Relationship effect:** Dan must let Caide lead in a domain where rank fails him.  
**Next consequence:** Sol will assess both the request and the man challenging his father.

#### Reader knowledge and continuity
**What the reader must understand:** Tahan is the king's uncle, the city's greatest Sage, and a popular hero.  
**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** Caide recognizes a version of himself in Tahan without understanding every aspect of his rage.  
**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** The history behind the entry phrase can be suggested through response rather than a new war subplot.  
**Earlier setup paid off:** The request chosen in Chapter 12; Caide's former reputation.  
**Later event prepared:** The duel, Tahan's self-augmentation, and Sol's role in containing him.  
**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** Tahan's Hiyang is Judgement, applied to himself; Caide's lost arm still changes his fighting options.

#### Scene list
1. **Location:** Estate gate | **Objective:** Obtain a hearing | **Resistance:** Dan's standing is insufficient | **Outcome:** Caide's reference opens the gate.
2. **Location:** Receiving room | **Objective:** Secure aid | **Resistance:** Tahan's contempt and demand for proof | **Outcome:** Caide accepts a duel.

#### Closing beat
**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** Caide faces the kind of soldier he still wants to be.

#### One sentence summary
Because Dan cannot obtain Tahan's cooperation through rank or argument, Caide uses his war history to gain entry and accepts a duel that turns the request for help into a personal test.
