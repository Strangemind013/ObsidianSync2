**Chapter number and working title:** Chapter 19 — A Victory That Needed Everyone  
**POV:** Caide.  
**Time and location:** A prepared confrontation site in Meroun; after the training period.  
**Essential characters:** Caide, Grin, Sol, the three companions, all seven Smokesons.  
**Approximate word budget:** 4,000 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

#### Purpose
**Main contribution to the book:** Deliver a messy collective success while exposing Caide's discomfort with dependence.  
**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** His jade choice would lack its immediate emotional pressure and the final coordinated fight would lack contrast.

#### Character motivation
**Immediate goal:** Capture Grin and prove he can still lead a meaningful victory.  
**Underlying motive:** Regain the identity of the hero who ends the danger.  
**Stakes:** The crew, the city, and his belief that he personally remains essential.

#### Dramatic question
**Question driving the chapter:** Can their practiced plan contain Grin?  
**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** They capture him despite mistakes, but Grin's intended surrender means this does not prove true containment.

#### Opposition and tension
**External resistance:** Timing failures, battle pressure, Grin's power, and Caide's impulse to compensate alone.  
**What the opposing character wants:** Grin secretly wants to be captured for his larger plan.  
**Internal pressure or competing desire:** Caide wants the team to succeed and still wishes success could feel like his old individual triumphs.

#### Causal sequence
**Starting situation:** Caide checks the assignments and reassures men who have trained with him.  
**Trigger:** They commit to the capture attempt.  
**First approach:** The ballista-and-seal plan begins.  
**Complication:** One timing error forces another teammate to improvise; Caide's solo correction is insufficient.  
**Response:** The crew covers the gaps and Sol completes enough suppression to permit capture.  
**Turning point:** Grin yields, leaving Caide with both gratitude and an unsettling sense that it was too easy.

#### Decision and action
**Options the character perceives:** Chase a personal opening or trust the recovery work others are doing.  
**Cost or risk of each option:** Solo action risks breaking the plan; trust means needing help openly.  
**Decision:** Let the collective effort finish, despite the sting to his pride.  
**Action taken:** Support the final restraint rather than taking every decisive role.  
**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** Custody and reward decisions occur in Chapter 20.

#### Outcome and change
**Practical result:** Grin enters custody; the team is celebrated.  
**Before to after:** Underprepared volunteers → credited victors with a misleading sense of safety.  
**Character interpretation:** Caide appreciates them while rationalizing that two hands would prevent future dependence.  
**Relationship effect:** The bond is real; his insecurity has not disappeared.  
**Next consequence:** The reward choice offers a shortcut to the restoration he still craves.

#### Reader knowledge and continuity
**What the reader must understand:** Grin wanted capture. The crew's courage and skill are real even though he permits the outcome.  
**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** Caide notices inconsistencies but does not yet know pain has made captivity useful to Grin.  
**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** Grin's intent remains concealed through limited POV; plant observable yielding or missing resistance.  
**Earlier setup paid off:** Training, assigned roles, and Sol's suppression.  
**Later event prepared:** Jade versus pardons; torture; the later Faceless fight executes this choreography correctly.  
**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** Ballista anchors an external restraint; Sol's companions lay seals. Define how these physically constrain Grin.

**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** The city calls them heroes while Caide remembers the moments when he could do nothing without them.

#### One sentence summary
Because the crew finally has a capture plan, Caide leads it against Grin, but mistakes force everyone to contribute to a victory that Grin secretly intends to allow.
