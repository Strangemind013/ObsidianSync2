Like every other morning, Caide stared down the insurmountable challenge of getting dressed with one arm. 

Every shirt he owns got two sleeves. He only needed one, and the other hung floppy at his side. He worked the cloth around with his fingers in a clumsy knot.

Putting on trousers consists of a messy tangle of legs and fabric. He tried doing it seated, but the problem was made obvious once he worked up his thighs. Standing up undid all his progress. He considered wearing a skirt.

Belts, boots, and then the straps that held his sword (a prop). Each their own struggle.

He didn’t own a mirror, but he imagined how goofy he must’ve looked, all askew. No matter, he thought, the cloak will cover everything anyway. 

He put on the cloak, and headed out. There was a bump under his boot, but he couldn’t be bothered.
