The minister spoke inane pleasantries, but Dan just heard *move it along, fat boy.* There was nothing thankful about this unannounced visit. No one even offered him tea! Dan would bet everything he had that the minister considers this a waste of time. The Minister is being insulting without forgetting his etiquette.

"You haven't even asked who I am."

"With all due respect, Minister. I am his adopted son."

"Then my apologies to your father, and to you, for missing the adoption ceremony. Please, you must help me seek forgivenes."

"Yes, your excellency, I must admit that you're right. I am not his son yet. I think he's hoping to get some unfortunate young woman with child, so she can pop out a legitimate heir. I wish him the best, and I honestly do hope it happens. But in the mean time, I'm afraid he's stuck with me."

"That's almost all of them. May I ask for what purpose?"

"I'm afraid not, your excellency," Dan said with bow.

Minister Sung's nose flared. "I'm not aware of what you think is happening here, young steward, but if you think you can come into my home and disrespect me... I will not let some criminal son of a whore--"

"Disrespect? Your excellency, you haven't even deigned to offer me tea. The only insult you've withheld was letting me through your gates."

"The answer is no, steward. Leave before my patience runs out."

"I see. Then you were right, your excellency. I did come here to argue about succession. Just not my father's."

The minister feigned confusion, but he could not hide the way he slightly went pale. "I already have an heir."

"You're about to make a point, young steward. Kindly get on with it."
