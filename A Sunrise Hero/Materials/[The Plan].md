# 🌅 DAILY WRITING SCHEDULE (Repeat 6 days/week)

You can adjust the times, but keep the structure.

---

## 🧠 Phase 1: Warm-Up (20 minutes)

**Goal:** Enter writing mode without pressure.

- 5 min: Re-read yesterday’s writing (no editing, just read)
    
- 5 min: Write 3 bullet points about today’s scene
    
- 10 min: Free-write in the world (character thoughts, lore, dialogue snippets)
    

No perfection allowed.

---

## ✍️ Phase 2: Deep Writing Block (60–90 minutes)

**Goal: 800–1,200 words per day**

- Timer set for 25 minutes
    
- 5 minute break
    
- 25 minutes
    
- 5 minute break
    
- Optional final 25 minutes if momentum is strong
    

Rules:

- No editing
    
- No rereading
    
- No research mid-session (write [RESEARCH THIS] and continue)
    
- Phone in another room
    

---

## 🧩 Phase 3: Micro-Planning (10 minutes)

Answer:

- What happens tomorrow?
    
- What emotional shift occurs?
    
- What conflict escalates?
    

Write tomorrow’s first sentence before stopping.

This eliminates next-day resistance.

---

## 📊 Daily Target

If you write:

- 1,000 words/day
    
- 6 days/week
    

You’ll produce:

- 6,000 words/week
    
- ~24,000 words/month
    
- A 90,000-word fantasy novel in ~4 months
    

Even 800/day finishes a book this year.

# 📅 WEEKLY STRUCTURE

## Monday–Friday

Draft new material.

## Saturday

Light session:

- 500 words OR
    
- Worldbuilding OR
    
- Character deepening OR
    
- Plot outlining next arc
    

## Sunday (No Drafting)

- Read 30–60 minutes in your genre
    
- Reflect on structure, pacing, magic systems
    
- Rest your brain
    

Rest is strategic, not laziness.

# 🗺️ PHASED NOVEL ROADMAP

This prevents wandering (a common fantasy problem).

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1–2)

- Main character defined
    
- Core conflict clear
    
- Magic system rules established
    
- Villain motivation understood
    
- Rough 3-act structure outlined
    

Do not over-outline. Just milestones.

---

## Phase 2: Act 1 (Weeks 3–6)

Goal: 25,000 words

- Introduce world
    
- Inciting incident
    
- First major turning point
    

---

## Phase 3: Act 2 (Weeks 7–12)

Goal: 40,000–45,000 words

- Escalation
    
- Midpoint twist
    
- Stakes intensify
    
- Characters forced to change
    

---

## Phase 4: Act 3 (Weeks 13–16)

Goal: 20,000 words

- Confrontation
    
- Sacrifice
    
- Climax
    
- Resolution

# 🚫 Anti-Procrastination System

This is the most important part.

---

## 1. The 10-Minute Rule

If you don’t feel like writing:  
Write for just 10 minutes.

You are allowed to stop after.  
You rarely will.

---

## 2. Track Visible Progress

Use a simple tracker:

|Day|Words Written|Total Word Count|
|---|---|---|

Seeing accumulation kills procrastination.

---

## 3. “No Zero Days” Rule

You must write at least:

- 200 words minimum
    

Even on terrible days.

Momentum > motivation.

---

## 4. Public Accountability (Optional but Powerful)

Tell one person:  
“I’m finishing my novel by [date].”

External pressure works.

---

# 🔥 If You Want to Finish Faster

Hardcore version:

- 1,500 words/day
    
- 5 days/week
    
- Draft finished in ~12 weeks
    

---

# 🧙 Fantasy-Specific Tip

End every writing day on:

- A reveal
    
- A betrayal
    
- A discovery
    
- A danger
    

This makes you _excited_ to return tomorrow.
