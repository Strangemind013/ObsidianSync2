**Chapter number and working title:** Chapter 5 — A Crew No One Else Will Join  
**POV:** Dan.  
**Time and location:** Snowdrop Garden and the Fool's Mug; after the shipment burns.  
**Essential characters:** Dan, the princess, Caide; the warden through arrangements or a brief exchange.  
**Approximate word budget:** 2,600 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

### Purpose

**Main contribution to the book:** Turn the impossible assignment into a concrete recruitment plan and bring Caide back into motion.  
**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** The prisoners' release and Caide's leadership would arrive without a cause.

### Character motivation

**Immediate goal:** Secure a willing firefighting crew and persuade Caide to lead it.  
**Underlying motive:** Win back his father's approval while doing something useful for Meroun.  
**Stakes:** His chance at rehabilitation and the lives endangered by worsening fires.

### Dramatic question

**Question driving the chapter:** Can Dan build a team from the resources he still has?  
**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** Yes, by trading political favors and offering prisoners a route toward freedom.

### Opposition and tension

**External resistance:** Free citizens fear defying the Summer God; the warden needs a reason to cooperate; Caide has withdrawn.  
**What the opposing character wants:** The warden wants advantage and protection; Caide wants to avoid another humiliating failure.  
**Internal pressure or competing desire:** Dan must admit he needs help while still viewing the project as his route home.

### Causal sequence

**Starting situation:** Dan inventories his reduced assets with the princess.  
**Trigger:** Ordinary recruitment proves impossible or obviously futile.  
**First approach:** He identifies prisoners whose prospects make the risk worth considering.  
**Complication:** Release requires official cover, and leadership requires a dispirited Caide.  
**Response:** Dan arranges the warden's cooperation and approaches Caide through purpose and pride.  
**Turning point:** Caide supplies dossiers and agrees to lead.

### Decision and action

**Options the character perceives:** Wait for respectable volunteers or recruit from prison.  
**Cost or risk of each option:** Waiting wastes the summer; recruitment exposes vulnerable men to dangerous, religiously condemned work.  
**Decision:** Proceed with covered release and a clear offer.  
**Action taken:** Choose candidates and organize the extraction.  
**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** The prisoners decide whether to accept in Chapter 6.

### Outcome and change

**Practical result:** The team exists as an actionable plan.  
**Before to after:** Punished official with no staff → organizer with a leader and recruitment route.  
**Character interpretation:** Dan believes he has found the test's answer.  
**Relationship effect:** The princess becomes a confidante; Caide accepts purpose from a friend he hurt.  
**Next consequence:** The prison extraction can happen without an active manhunt.

### Reader knowledge and continuity

**What the reader must understand:** The release is arranged with the warden and any necessary House of Order authority.  
**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** Dan knows he cannot guarantee a royal pardon merely by promising one.  
**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** The exact favors need not be dramatized, but the terms should be clear enough to avoid a later fugitive subplot.  
**Earlier setup paid off:** Dan's administrative network; Caide's loss of hope.  
**Later event prepared:** The seven Smokesons, their pardon expectations, and Dan's talent for mobilizing others.  
**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** House of Order oversees prisons; religious fear makes ordinary recruitment difficult.

### Scene list

1. **Location:** Snowdrop Garden | **Objective:** Find usable resources | **Resistance:** Cash shortage and social exclusion | **Outcome:** Prison recruitment becomes the viable route.
2. **Location:** Fool's Mug | **Objective:** Recruit a leader | **Resistance:** Caide's despair | **Outcome:** Caide accepts and produces dossiers.

### Closing beat

**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** The dossiers become a list of men Dan must persuade rather than merely acquire.

### One sentence summary

Because respectable volunteers will not defy the Summer God, Dan arranges a covered prison release and recruits Caide to turn desperate men into a firefighting crew.
