**Chapter number and working title:** Chapter 26 — A Target Without Enough Light  
**POV:** Seyla.  
**Time and location:** The same nighttime battlefield; continuous with Chapter 25.  
**Essential characters:** Seyla, Caide, Sol, the three companions, the Smokesons, the Faceless.  
**Approximate word budget:** 2,400 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

### Purpose

**Main contribution to the book:** Give the coordinated plan its successful payoff while revealing the final missing resource.  
**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** The climax could confuse a good plan with incompetence or make Seyla's finishing beam effortless.

### Character motivation

**Immediate goal:** Destroy the Faceless before the restraint fails.  
**Underlying motive:** Apply her existing experience to save this group rather than merely display power.  
**Stakes:** Everyone holding the position and people beyond it.

### Dramatic question

**Question driving the chapter:** Can a successfully restrained Faceless be destroyed with the power available tonight?  
**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** The target is held correctly, but Seyla lacks the light needed for a decisive beam.

### Opposition and tension

**External resistance:** Regeneration exceeds damage; it is night; maintaining the restraint costs effort.  
**What the opposing character wants:** The creature is trying to break free and continue attacking.  
**Internal pressure or competing desire:** Seyla must communicate a limit while people are depending on her.

### Causal sequence

**Starting situation:** Seyla arrives as the team secures or completes the restraint.  
**Trigger:** She recognizes the regeneration problem from prior experience.  
**First approach:** She uses her Light-Break-Destiny beam.  
**Complication:** Its current output cannot destroy the creature faster than it repairs.  
**Response:** She identifies the missing resource while the men hold their assigned jobs.  
**Turning point:** She tells Caide exactly what kind of light and timing would make another shot viable.

### Decision and action

**Options the character perceives:** Repeat inadequate attacks, withdraw, or ask for a resource she cannot currently supply.  
**Cost or risk of each option:** Repeating wastes strength; withdrawal exposes others; asking requires others to maintain a dangerous hold.  
**Decision:** State the limitation and define a usable opening.  
**Action taken:** Prepare the beam and coordinate a timed opportunity.  
**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** Caide supplies the flash in Chapter 27.

### Outcome and change

**Practical result:** The team proves it can restrain the enemy; the killing method remains one resource short.  
**Before to after:** Uncertain plan → functioning plan with a precise bottleneck.  
**Character interpretation:** Seyla sees a shared solution becoming possible.  
**Relationship effect:** Caide must complement her capability rather than replace her as the finisher.  
**Next consequence:** A brief intense light source can decide the fight.

### Reader knowledge and continuity

**What the reader must understand:** This is the clean execution promised by the training contrast; it should look better than the Grin capture.  
**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** Seyla knows more about Faceless than this group; check her other plotline before assigning additional history.  
**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** Her relevant knowledge should be spoken; mystery about origins need not conceal the practical weakness.  
**Earlier setup paid off:** Ballista, seal placement, suppression, and her established signature beam.  
**Later event prepared:** The timed flash and final shot.  
**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** Light creates light, so darkness alone is insufficient explanation. Define a cost/output limit or current depletion that prevents self-supplying the required intensity.

### Scene list

2. **Location:** Firing position | **Objective:** Deliver the finishing beam | **Resistance:** Insufficient usable light | **Outcome:** She communicates a precise requirement while the hold continues.

### Closing beat

**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** The enemy is finally where they need it; Seyla tells Caide what is still missing.

### One sentence summary

Because the crew successfully holds the Faceless, Seyla tries to destroy it, but insufficient usable light forces her to coordinate a brief stronger source with Caide.
