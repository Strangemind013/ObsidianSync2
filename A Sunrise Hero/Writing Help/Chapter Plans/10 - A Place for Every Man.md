**Chapter number and working title:** Chapter 10 — A Place for Every Man

**POV:** Lon — proposed assignment to give the equipment-focused prisoner a chapter.

**Time and location:** The crew's base and a successful fire response; after Duck's injury.

**Essential characters:** Lon, Duck, Caide, Dan, the crew; Grin at the end.

**Approximate word budget:** 2,200 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

## Purpose

**Main contribution to the book:** Let the crew experience earned belonging and practical adaptation before Grin confronts them.

**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** Their rising confidence would be asserted rather than felt, and Duck's changed life would be skipped.

## Character motivation

**Immediate goal:** Lon: solve an equipment problem that keeps a teammate participating.

**Underlying motive:** Prove that useful work need not depend on noble standing or sagecraft.

**Stakes:** Duck's place in the crew and Lon's own sense that he contributes.

## Dramatic question

**Question driving the chapter:** Can the crew make its success sustainable for the men doing the work?

**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** They make practical progress, just before Grin reveals the larger danger.

## Opposition and tension

**External resistance:** Duck must adapt to a peg leg; equipment is crude; support remains conditional.

**What the opposing character wants:** Duck wants usefulness without being patronized; Dan wants the operation to continue.

**Internal pressure or competing desire:** Lon's grumbling can conceal anxiety about his own replaceability.

## Causal sequence

**Starting situation:** The crew has begun receiving praise and using its new support.

**Trigger:** Duck returns with a peg leg and a changed range of tasks.

**First approach:** Lon helps adapt a cart, tool, or work routine.

**Complication:** A practical limitation defeats the first attempt; authority from Cardinale's circle is still threatening.

**Response:** They find a role that Duck can actually perform and complete another useful response.

**Turning point:** Grin appears and orders them to stop.

## Decision and action

**Options the character perceives:** Treat Duck as an invalid, pretend nothing changed, or adapt the work.

**Cost or risk of each option:** Exclusion humiliates; denial risks injury; adaptation requires cooperation.

**Decision:** Proposed: change the task and equipment.

**Action taken:** Make one adjustment on page, with Duck directing what helps.

**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** The confrontation erupts in Chapter 11.

## Outcome and change

**Practical result:** The men briefly feel like a functioning crew.

**Before to after:** Disposable recruits → people making room for one another.

**Character interpretation:** Lon can see practical skill as a contribution worth claiming.

**Relationship effect:** Duck's humor and the nickname exchange show affection without curing him.

**Next consequence:** Grin's arrival threatens the enterprise itself.

## Reader knowledge and continuity

**What the reader must understand:** Duck's peg leg is not presented as proof that Caide should stop wanting an arm.

**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** Lon can judge the team's behavior, not Caide's unspoken feelings.

**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** Grin's nature is unknown to the crew until he demonstrates it.

**Earlier setup paid off:** Duck's injury and the compensation/equipment system.

**Later event prepared:** The cart-mounted ballista; its builder being Lon is a proposal, not settled lore.

**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** Dan already knows Caide is a Sage. Remove the obsolete discovery beat.

## Closing beat

**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** Grin addresses the crew directly, turning their growing reputation into a threat.

## One sentence summary

Because Duck's injury changes how he can work, Lon helps the crew adapt its equipment, but their renewed confidence ends when Grin arrives to forbid further firefighting.
