**Chapter number and working title:** Chapter 3 — An Impossible Assignment  
**POV:** Dan.  
**Time and location:** Cardinale's household or Works offices, then the Fool's Mug.  
**Essential characters:** Dan, Minister Cardinale, household senior staff.  
**Approximate word budget:** 2,600 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

### Purpose

**Main contribution to the book:** Strip Dan of borrowed authority and set the father-son conflict in motion.  
**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** The firefighting assignment would feel voluntary rather than a desperate attempt to recover belonging.

### Character motivation

**Immediate goal:** Defend his grain arrangement and retain his father's confidence.  
**Underlying motive:** Prove that everything he has done serves Cardinale.  
**Stakes:** Income, residence, influence, and the recognition Dan hopes to earn.

### Dramatic question

**Question driving the chapter:** Can Dan explain his good intentions well enough to preserve his position?  
**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** No; his father redefines initiative as insubordination.

### Opposition and tension

**External resistance:** Cardinale controls the meeting, the household, and Dan's delegated authority.  
**What the opposing character wants:** Reassert control and make Dan's independence costly.  
**Internal pressure or competing desire:** Dan cannot easily condemn the man whose approval defines his worth.

### Causal sequence

**Starting situation:** Dan arrives expecting ordinary business.  
**Trigger:** The assembled senior staff reveal this is a public reckoning.  
**First approach:** He explains the grain plan and its intended credit.  
**Complication:** His father treats the unauthorized commitment as disloyal overreach.  
**Response:** Dan seeks private reassurance after the public demotion.  
**Turning point:** Cardinale leaves him the summer-fire assignment and the inadequate pub.

### Decision and action

**Options the character perceives:** Defy the accusation, abandon service, or accept the assignment.  
**Cost or risk of each option:** Defiance risks total exclusion; leaving abandons his hoped-for future; acceptance means tackling an apparently impossible task.  
**Decision:** Accept and seek redemption.  
**Action taken:** Move operations to the Fool's Mug and assess his resources.  
**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** A workable response develops in Chapter 5.

### Outcome and change

**Practical result:** Dan loses office, support, and ready income.  
**Before to after:** Trusted representative → publicly sidelined dependent.  
**Character interpretation:** He interprets the assignment as a test he can pass.  
**Relationship effect:** The father's control deepens while Dan still calls it guidance.  
**Next consequence:** Cash tied up in grain and Caide's payment leaves little room to maneuver.

### Reader knowledge and continuity

**What the reader must understand:** Dan helped raise Cardinale from a middling noble to Minister of Works.  
**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** Dan knows the punishment is unfair but still assumes loyalty can repair it.  
**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** The father's absolute refusal to accept a common-born heir need not be stated yet; his behavior must support the later revelation.  
**Earlier setup paid off:** Chapter 1's overreach; Chapter 2's expense.  
**Later event prepared:** The crew, the pub headquarters, and Dan's recurring need to act without his father's authorization.  
**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** Removal of delegated authority differs from confiscation of Dan's private grain.

### Scene list

1. **Location:** Public meeting | **Objective:** Defend his conduct | **Resistance:** Cardinale controls the accusation | **Outcome:** Demotion announced.
2. **Location:** Private exchange and departure | **Objective:** Recover reassurance | **Resistance:** Conditional affection and no real concession | **Outcome:** Dan accepts the fire assignment.

### Closing beat

**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** Dan reaches the Fool's Mug and begins turning the punishment into a plan.

### One sentence summary

Because an official complains about his unauthorized commitments, Dan tries to justify the grain reserve, but his father removes his authority and assigns him the summer fires.
