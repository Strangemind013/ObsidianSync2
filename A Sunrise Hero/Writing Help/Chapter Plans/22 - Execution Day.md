**Chapter number and working title:** Chapter 22 — Execution Day  
**POV:** Hidar, as in the original outline; his wider role needs checking against the other plotline.  
**Time and location:** The public execution ground; roughly a week after the interrupted wedding.  
**Essential characters:** Hidar, Grin, Cardinale, Tahan, execution officials and spectators.  
**Approximate word budget:** 3,500 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

### Purpose

**Main contribution to the book:** Deliver the price of Dan's bargain and destroy the city's confidence in its protectors.  
**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** Dan's rise would evade its actual cost, and Grin's captivity would have no payoff.

### Character motivation

**Immediate goal:** Hidar: protect people when the execution becomes an attack.  
**Underlying motive:** Act according to his established loyalties, which are not specified in these notes.  
**Stakes:** His life, Cardinale's life, and the city's surviving protection.

### Dramatic question

**Question driving the chapter:** Can the city's defenders stop the prisoner they believe defeated?  
**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** No; Grin uses his accumulated power to kill Tahan and fulfill the assassination bargain.

### Opposition and tension

**External resistance:** Grin has become vastly stronger under torture.  
**What the opposing character wants:** Kill the agreed targets and generate the despair needed for his lover.  
**Internal pressure or competing desire:** Hidar must choose how to act when resistance appears hopeless.

### Causal sequence

**Starting situation:** A bloodied Grin is brought out to be poisoned.  
**Trigger:** He smiles and burns through his restraints.  
**First approach:** Hidar protects Cardinale from the first targeted attack.  
**Complication:** Tahan joins the defense, but Grin kills the city's greatest Sage.  
**Response:** Hidar must continue protecting, evacuate others, or heed Grin's offer to stand aside.  
**Turning point:** The defense collapses; Cardinale must also die for the agreed end-state.

### Decision and action

**Options the character perceives:** Continue a losing defense or redirect effort toward people he can still save.  
**Cost or risk of each option:** Fighting may cost Hidar his life; withdrawal leaves the targeted minister exposed.  
**Decision:** Open: choose according to Hidar's own arc, not merely Dan's plot needs.  
**Action taken:** The original has Hidar fall; define whether this is injury, defeat, or death.  
**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** The city's devastation continues through Chapters 23–28.

### Outcome and change

**Practical result:** Tahan dies; Grin escapes; Cardinale's death must be explicitly placed.  
**Before to after:** Public execution as reassurance → public proof of helplessness.  
**Character interpretation:** Hidar's interpretation depends on his established beliefs; do not invent them here.  
**Relationship effect:** Sol loses his father; Meroun loses its central heroic protector.  
**Next consequence:** Dan's expected opportunity arrives through a catastrophe he helped shape.

### Reader knowledge and continuity

**What the reader must understand:** Torture empowered Grin; the execution did not suddenly grant him strength.  
**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** Hidar knows the immediate defense, not necessarily Dan's deal.  
**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** If the reader saw the bargain, suspense concerns its cost; if not, a fair earlier setup is still required.  
**Earlier setup paid off:** The intended capture, prison pain, Dan's information, and Tahan's public stature.  
**Later event prepared:** Sol's grief, the power vacuum, Dan's succession, and later distrust.  
**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** Grin's Warmth mastery must account for melting restraints and the killing strike; exact defenses need choreography.

### Closing beat

**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** The crowd sees the city's strongest protector dead where it expected proof of safety.

### One sentence summary

Because the authorities mistake capture for containment, Grin uses his torture-fed power to escape execution and kill the men Dan named, turning a public reassurance into mass despair.
