**Chapter number and working title:** Chapter 14 — Two Negotiations  
**POV:** Dan.  
**Time and location:** Tahan's training ground, immediately after Chapter 13.  
**Essential characters:** Dan, Sol, Caide, Tahan; Sol's three companions.  
**Approximate word budget:** 3,000 words, provisional; the whole-book allocation is not fixed.

### Purpose

**Main contribution to the book:** Establish Sol as an accomplished, necessary ally while Dan negotiates independently of the martial spectacle.  
**What would be lost if this chapter were removed:** Sol would feel like a lesser substitute rather than the specialist the crew needs.

### Character motivation

**Immediate goal:** Secure useful assistance regardless of the duel's result.  
**Underlying motive:** Show that he can build an alliance rather than merely borrow another father's power.  
**Stakes:** The city's defense and the credibility of his new undertaking.

### Dramatic question

**Question driving the chapter:** Can Dan obtain help while Caide fights to earn it?  
**Answer or meaningful progress provided:** Yes; the duel earns respect, and Sol's specialization makes him the practical answer.

### Opposition and tension

**External resistance:** Sol questions the plan and the risk of teaching prisoners sagecraft.  
**What the opposing character wants:** Protect his father and avoid committing his companions to a reckless mission.  
**Internal pressure or competing desire:** Dan wants to sell certainty he does not possess.

### Causal sequence

**Starting situation:** Caide and Tahan begin their agreed duel.  
**Trigger:** Dan sees Sol watching for more than who wins.  
**First approach:** He pitches the crew's objective and their shared interest in stopping Grin.  
**Complication:** Sol challenges the plan's limits while Tahan's augmentation intensifies.  
**Response:** Dan offers a defined role, support, and realistic terms; Sol demonstrates or explains suppression.  
**Turning point:** The duel ends in a tie, and Sol is assigned or agrees to help in Tahan's place.

### Decision and action

**Options the character perceives:** Inflate the plan, appeal to status, or admit the missing capability.  
**Cost or risk of each option:** Bluffing may win agreement but endanger everyone; honesty weakens the sales pitch but enables a real alliance.  
**Decision:** Proposed: state what the crew cannot do.  
**Action taken:** Negotiate for Sol's exact suppression role and his companions' support.  
**If the chapter breaks before this is completed, where it continues:** Their integration begins in Chapter 15.

### Outcome and change

**Practical result:** The crew gains Sol and three trained companions.  
**Before to after:** Seeking overwhelming power → acquiring the right capability.  
**Character interpretation:** Dan learns that apparent lesser status need not mean lesser practical value.  
**Relationship effect:** Sol respects a workable concession; Caide earns Tahan's martial regard.  
**Next consequence:** Training can now build toward a combined restraint plan.

### Reader knowledge and continuity

**What the reader must understand:** Sol is already accomplished; he routinely suppresses his father's rage during overuse.  
**What the POV character knows or misunderstands:** Dan can observe the duel, not narrate Caide's inner struggle.  
**What remains concealed and why the concealment is fair:** Sol's concerns should be stated rather than hidden to manufacture mistrust.  
**Earlier setup paid off:** The challenge and Chapter 12's need for suppression.  
**Later event prepared:** Area gravity, seal placement, coded stud nudges, and the Grin capture.  
**Relevant worldbuilding or magic rules:** Judgement augments Tahan's own body; Sol affects external gravity. Do not directly enchant another living person's muscles.

### Scene list

1. **Location:** Training-ground edge | **Objective:** Secure Sol's commitment | **Resistance:** Doubts about the prisoners and the plan | **Outcome:** Dan defines a credible role.
2. **Location:** Duel's conclusion | **Objective:** Confirm the alliance | **Resistance:** Tahan's pride and martial intensity | **Outcome:** Draw acknowledged; Sol joins the response.

### Closing beat

**Final action, exchange, image, discovery, or unresolved development:** Dan leaves with Sol's help, having learned why the strongest fighter was not necessarily the missing piece.

### One sentence summary

Because Tahan tests Caide through combat, Dan uses the duel to negotiate with Sol, whose suppression expertise turns martial respect into a usable alliance.
